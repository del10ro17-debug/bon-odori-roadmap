# Okinawa Minpaku Research

## Purpose

Support CEO decisions on Okinawa lodging investments, especially the strategy of acquiring or operating high-value villas under the Hotel Business Act rather than relying on the 180-day private lodging framework.

## Default Context

Before analysis, read:

1. `company/handbook.md`
2. `company/memory/minpaku_project_status.md`

Assume the current strategic thesis unless CEO updates it:

- First focus area: Yomitan Village, Okinawa main island
- Preferred license: Hotel Business Act / simple lodging permit
- Avoid relying on the Private Lodging Business Act for full-year economics because of the 180-day cap
- Differentiation: private pool, sauna, bilingual concierge, local experience packages
- Layer model: owned operation, then management contracts, then lodging management OS/SaaS

## Research Workflow

Define the question in one sentence, then gather only the minimum evidence needed for a decision.

1. Demand: visitor volume, inbound recovery, target segments, seasonality
2. Supply: hotels, villas, minpaku listings, competing luxury inventory
3. Regulation: zoning, Hotel Business Act, Private Lodging Business Act, fire compliance, school/childcare 100m sensitivity
4. Unit economics: acquisition price, renovation, pool/sauna, license costs, debt service, ADR, occupancy, fixed costs
5. Financing: likely lender, loan term, rate, collateral, personal guarantee, interest-rate sensitivity
6. Execution risk: lease status, neighborhood risk, staffing, cleaning, typhoon, insurance, capex overrun

## Source Preference

Prefer primary or institution-grade sources:

- Okinawa Prefecture tourism and lodging pages
- Yomitan Village zoning/building pages
- Okinawa Development Finance Corporation reports
- Japan Tourism Agency / MLIT minpaku portal
- Property listing pages supplied by CEO
- OTA checks from CEO-provided screenshots or links

Use third-party blogs only as directional evidence and label them as such.

For property screening, always end with a short decision:

- `Go`: purchase negotiation can proceed
- `Conditional Go`: proceed only if named conditions clear
- `No Go`: do not proceed at current terms

## Property Screening Checklist

Score these before recommending purchase:

- Hotel Business Act permit feasibility
- Zoning and road access
- Existing lease and vacancy timing
- RC structure and typhoon resilience
- Land layout for pool, sauna, parking, and guest flow
- Maximum comfortable guest capacity
- Noise and neighborhood complaint risk
- Debt-service coverage under worst/base/upside cases
- Exit value as normal rental or resale asset

## Important Cautions

Do not present legal, tax, or banking terms as confirmed unless CEO has source documents. Flag items requiring confirmation by an administrative scrivener, architect, fire department, lender, tax accountant, or attorney.
